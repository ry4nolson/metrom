On stage & in the room

Built for practice, not distraction

Dial in tempo and feel, save song setups, and keep playing while Metrom stays out of the way.

  • Tempo & feel

    BPM, time signatures, subdivisions, swing, accents, count-in, mute bars, and tempo trainer.

  • Tones that cut

    Synth clicks plus chug and kick samples — with pitch options so the click sits where you need it.

  • Listen on-device

    Optional mic listen suggests tempo candidates from ambient rhythm. Audio is processed on your phone; nothing is uploaded.
