Support

We’re here to help with Metrom on iOS and Android.

For bug reports, feature questions, or store-related issues, email us. Include your device model, OS version, and what you were doing when the problem occurred when you can.

Email

support@metrom.dev

Common topics

  • Microphone / Listen — Metrom needs mic permission only for tempo listen. Audio stays on your device. Check system Settings if Listen is unavailable.
  • Background playback — Keep Metrom’s audio session active via the system media controls; some devices may pause audio under aggressive battery settings.
  • Privacy — See our Privacy Policy for what Metrom does and does not collect.
  • Terms — Terms of Use for the apps and this site.

Store listings

After Metrom is live, you can also reach support through the App Store or Google Play listing contact links, which point here.
